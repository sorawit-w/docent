# Docent — Descriptions

## Tagline

> A guide inside every document.

## Short bio (60 characters or fewer)

Design kit for documents that guide the reader.

## Medium bio (160 characters or fewer)

Docent is a design kit for self-contained HTML guides and reports. It adds step-by-step walkthroughs and moving workflow diagrams, in light and dark.

## Long bio (280 characters or fewer)

Docent makes long documents easier to follow. It gives self-contained HTML guides and reports one calm look, walkthroughs that light the current step, and workflow diagrams that show the path. It works in light and dark, prints cleanly, and belongs to no single project.

## Elevator pitch (about 75 words)

Long technical documents are hard to follow. The reader has to find the path alone. Docent is a design kit that puts a guide inside the document. A walkthrough lights one step at a time and dims the rest. A workflow diagram shows the order with a moving dot. Every document is one self-contained file. It opens in light or dark, prints cleanly, and stays readable without scripts. Docent belongs to no single project, so any team can use it.
